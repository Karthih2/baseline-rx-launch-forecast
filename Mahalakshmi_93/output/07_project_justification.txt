--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Selected model:
BASS-ONLY


MODEL SELECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The project originally considered six forecasting models.

Naive and ARIMA were excluded from the final candidate selection
according to the project decision.

The remaining four candidate models were:

1. Analog-only
2. Bass-only
3. Analog + Bass (Static)
4. Analog + Bass (Adaptive)

Leakage-safe validation was performed using:

Seeds:
93400 and 94721

Cutoffs:
6, 9, 12 and 18 months

Forecast horizon:
6 months

Future leakage:
OFF


FINAL FOUR-MODEL PERFORMANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Bass-only:
MASE  = 2.0371
MAPE  = 8.8991%
RMSE  = 16329.43

Analog + Bass (Static):
MASE  = 5.2422

Analog + Bass (Adaptive):
MASE  = 5.8763

Analog-only:
MASE  = 10.6769


WHY BASS-ONLY WAS SELECTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Bass-only achieved the lowest MASE among the four permitted models.

Therefore it was selected using:

Primary metric:
MASE

Tie breaker:
RMSE

Second tie breaker:
sMAPE

SEED SELECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Seed 93400 is used as the PRIMARY forecast dataset.

Reason:
It produced the lower historical Bass MASE and MAPE.

Seed 94721 is retained as the ROBUSTNESS dataset.

Important:
The two seeds generate different NEW_001 product instances and
different early-Rx trajectories. Therefore their forecasts are
not averaged together.

Seed 93400 is used for the final product forecast.


NEW_001 DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary seed:
93400

Drug:
New Drug X Nurocort

Early weekly observations:
22

The weekly observations were converted into monthly observations
using four-week groups. A partial final month uses the available
observations.


FINAL FORECAST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The final forecast is generated using Bass-only and the available
early-Rx history of NEW_001 from the primary seed.

No future validation observations are used during fitting.


INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Bass-only was selected because it was the strongest model among the
four allowed candidates under leakage-safe validation.

The model also showed:

- Lowest overall MASE
- Reasonable seed consistency
- 0% fallback usage
- Competitive performance across multiple history cutoffs
- No use of future validation observations during model fitting


IMPORTANT LIMITATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NEW_001 has a short early-Rx history.

Therefore the 12-month forecast should be interpreted as a model-based
scenario forecast rather than a guaranteed future outcome.
