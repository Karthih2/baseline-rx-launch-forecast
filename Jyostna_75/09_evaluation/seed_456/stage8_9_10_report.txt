[Analog_Only] -> POSSIBLE OVERFITTING
    - LOO MASE is unstable across the 35 analogs (std/mean = 0.7448) -- performance depends heavily on which analog is held out.

[Bass_Only] -> GOOD GENERALIZATION
    - train-window walk-forward MASE (0.9648) and held-out backtest MASE (0.9258) are close (gap=-0.039), and LOO error is not concentrated in a handful of analogs -- the model performs consistently on data it wasn't fit to.

[Analog_Bass_Static] -> POSSIBLE OVERFITTING
    - LOO MASE is unstable across the 35 analogs (std/mean = 0.8032) -- performance depends heavily on which analog is held out.
    - a small subset of analogs (top 5 of 35: ['ANL_007', 'ANL_017', 'ANL_016', 'ANL_022', 'ANL_019']) account for 51.3% of this model's total LOO error -- the average score is being driven by a few bad folds, not a broadly generalizable fit.

[Analog_Bass_Adaptive] -> POSSIBLE OVERFITTING
    - LOO MASE is unstable across the 35 analogs (std/mean = 0.8032) -- performance depends heavily on which analog is held out.
    - a small subset of analogs (top 5 of 35: ['ANL_007', 'ANL_017', 'ANL_016', 'ANL_022', 'ANL_019']) account for 51.3% of this model's total LOO error -- the average score is being driven by a few bad folds, not a broadly generalizable fit.
    - EXTRA SCRUTINY (per spec): this model recalibrates every time a new week of early Rx arrives, so it has the most flexibility to fit noise on only 4-6 months of new-drug data.
    - the adaptive walk-forward log shows one-step-ahead error shrinking as more weeks are fed in -- consistent with genuine adaptation rather than noise-chasing.
