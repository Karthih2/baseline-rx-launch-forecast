STAGE 9 -- OVERFITTING / UNDERFITTING ANALYSIS
==============================================================================

[Naive] -> POSSIBLE UNDERFITTING
    - backtest MASE=1.0 and LOO out-of-sample MASE=1.0 are both ~1.0 (i.e. barely better than last-value-carryforward) -- the model isn't extracting usable signal beyond the naive baseline.

[ARIMA] -> GOOD GENERALIZATION
    - train-window walk-forward MASE (1.0499) and held-out backtest MASE (0.9357) are close (gap=-0.1142), and LOO error is not concentrated in a handful of analogs -- the model performs consistently on data it wasn't fit to.

[Analog_Only] -> POSSIBLE OVERFITTING
    - LOO MASE is unstable across the 35 analogs (std/mean = 0.7242) -- performance depends heavily on which analog is held out.

[Bass_Only] -> LOW OVERFITTING RISK
    - no strong overfitting or underfitting signal in either the train/backtest gap or LOO consistency check.

[Analog_Bass_Static] -> LOW OVERFITTING RISK
    - no strong overfitting or underfitting signal in either the train/backtest gap or LOO consistency check.

[Analog_Bass_Adaptive] -> LOW OVERFITTING RISK
    - no strong overfitting or underfitting signal in either the train/backtest gap or LOO consistency check.
    - EXTRA SCRUTINY (per spec): this model recalibrates every time a new week of early Rx arrives, so it has the most flexibility to fit noise on only 4-6 months of new-drug data.
    - the adaptive walk-forward log shows one-step-ahead error shrinking as more weeks are fed in -- consistent with genuine adaptation rather than noise-chasing.
